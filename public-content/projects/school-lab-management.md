# School Laboratory Management

**Status:** COMPLETED

## Overview

An optimized operating-system restoration environment designed for school laboratories, built on centralized OS cloning.

## What I Built

- Centralized OS cloning services (Clonezilla / FOG Project).
- PXE boot environment for network imaging.
- NFS/TFTP-based deployment of images.
- Automated registry patches to lock student accounts and prevent system file alteration.

## Outcome

Over 40 computers in a lab restored to pristine clean states in under 15 minutes, down from hours of manual reinstalls — directly supporting the digital laboratory operations managed across the institution.

## Technology

FOG Project, Clonezilla Server, PXE boot, NFS/TFTP, shell scripting.
