# Python-Based CSV/PDF Automation for Infrastructure Audits

**Category:** Automation · **5 min read**

Manual processing of audit spreadsheets from dozens of public school labs is highly prone to errors and takes hours. With Python, this process can be fully automated.

## What I covered

- Using Pandas to read survey CSV sheets and run data validation.
- Spotting hardware inventory deficits automatically.
- Outputting formatted, government-ready PDF reports with ReportLab.
- Linking a lightweight Telegram bot API to broadcast critical alerts directly to technical coordinators.

## Why it matters

Automation here wasn't about elegance — it was about correctness and hours. Deterministic scripts replaced error-prone manual work, cut 15+ hours weekly, and produced consistent, compliant reports.

## Technology

Python, Pandas, ReportLab, Telegram API.
